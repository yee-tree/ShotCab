does not contain OCR models or redistributable binaries. Its dependency and

Third-party code and assets used by the vendored ShareX libraries retain their
own terms; upstream notices are in `upstream/ShareX/Licenses`. Anyone building a
binary distribution should review and include the notices for the actual
components shipped with that build.
